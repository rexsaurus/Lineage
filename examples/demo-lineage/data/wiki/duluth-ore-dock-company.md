The **Duluth Ore Dock Company** is the employer named on the 1938 payroll ledger in this demonstration lineage. Ore trains
ran out along the top of its docks and dropped iron ore through chutes into the boats below.[^b][^s1]

## The Calders at the dock
In October 1938 the payroll for Dock No. 6 listed [[Anders Calder]] as dock foreman and his son [[Henrik Calder]] as chute
tender.[^p] Henrik's daughter [[Ruth Calder]] remembered the dock as so tall "you had to tip your head all the way back,"
with the trains running "right out on top of it."[^s1]

## History
A 1925 history of the docks at the head of Lake Superior, now in the public domain, describes the company's timber and
steel docks and their chutes.[^b]
