**Henrik Calder** (born 1910) was a Duluth ore-dock worker and the father of [[Ruth Calder]].[^c][^p]

## Life
He appears as an infant in his father's household in the 1910 census.[^c] In October 1938 he was a chute tender at Dock
No. 6 of the [[Duluth Ore Dock Company]], where his father was foreman.[^p] His daughter remembered carrying his lunch
there in a tin pail.[^s1]

## War
In September 1944 he wrote to Ruth from France, where he was serving with the Arrowhead Engineer Battalion; eleven of his
letters survive with his daughter [[June Calder Moe]].[^r4][^tl]
