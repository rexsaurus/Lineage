**Ruth Calder** (born 1938) is the subject of this demonstration lineage, an invented family used to show what Lineage
does. She grew up in Duluth, Minnesota, trained as a nurse in Minneapolis, and with her husband [[Walt]] built a cabin on
Pike Lake.[^s1a][^s2a]

## Early life
Ruth was born in Duluth in 1938, the daughter of [[Henrik Calder]], who worked at the ore docks.[^tl][^r3] When she was
"maybe seven or eight" she carried her father's lunch to the dock in a tin pail; the dock was so tall "you had to tip your
head all the way back."[^s1a] In November 1950 a great snowfall closed her school for a week, and she and her brother
[[Pete]] dug a tunnel from the porch to the street; the _Duluth Lakeside Courier_ reported the closing on its front
page.[^s1b][^r5] Her father wrote to her from France in September 1944.[^r4]

## Nursing and marriage
In 1956 she went to Minneapolis for three years of nursing school, living in a dormitory where the house mother inspected
the students' shoes every morning.[^s2a] She met Walt at a church dance in 1959.[^s2b]

## Pike Lake
From about 1964 Ruth and Walt built a cabin on Pike Lake over four summers. Walt built the walls and Ruth the roof,
"because he was afraid of heights."[^s2c]

## In the family's memory
> "The lake was always there. Whatever else happened, the lake was always there."[^s2d]
