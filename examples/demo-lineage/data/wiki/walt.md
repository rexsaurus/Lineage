**Walt** was the husband of [[Ruth Calder]]. They met at a church dance in Minneapolis in 1959; he "couldn't dance at
all" and asked to walk her home.[^s2a] From about 1964 the two built a cabin on Pike Lake over four summers; he built the
walls, and she built the roof because he was afraid of heights.[^s2b]
