v0.8.0 · 14 commits (916a53d…ab7d35c) · from the release history

  • Release 0.8.0: Copyright checks for published books; open Internet Archive downloads; encyclopedia articles for any subject; a digitization work spec
  • build_instance: --hide sources,settings for public snapshots (areas removed, not just hidden)
  • Familypedia: 'Sources from the book' (each story's #records list on every entry it touches); data/portraits.csv drives portrait slots, story cards and family-tree photos (illustrations allowed, labelled); site builds find Typst packages; key check no longer matches words like 'musk-ox'
  • Familypedia: the browse/search panel no longer stays pinned over the articles on narrow screens
  • No placeholders: where a person, thing or story has no image, show nothing (no initial, icon or ? box)
  • Public snapshots keep every image a page shows when Sources is hidden (the work/ folder was being deleted wholesale); a person's chosen image is their portrait even when it is also a chapter cover
  • Familypedia lists only family members when data/family_members.csv exists; story cards show the chapter's chosen image (no letter tiles)
  • Static snapshots can serve every image from Cloudflare R2 (--assets manifest): full size by path, ~480px thumbnails wherever a page shows images small; local copies dropped
  • Home: story of the day, stories, gallery (photographs first, no document scans), relatives index (one entry per family member, chosen portraits only); story openings skip layout code; recording citations in prose render as chips
  • Portrait chips everywhere (Familypedia lists, story and timeline links, tree, home); no type icons; family tree from the project's data/family_tree.csv when present (pill nodes with portraits, opens at reading size on the subject, click opens the Familypedia article); home lists everyone in the family tree
  • Curated encyclopedia articles (app/wiki.py: data/wiki/<name>.md with infobox, sections, numbered references) shown in Familypedia in place of the derived page; passages and book sources only when they name the subject; original-photos-only gallery (/api/engine/gallery: no illustrations, restorations, scans or near-duplicates); snapshot copies book-used images from skipped folders and waits longer for long chapters; readable citation excerpts
  • Familypedia: People, Events and Places tabs (portrait cards); article Contents box no longer pinned; encyclopedia articles use their own class (.encyc) so the old two-column .wiki grid no longer scrambles headings and paragraphs; every picture labelled (original), (illustration) or (digital restoration) on the gallery and Photographs; static snapshots centre cropped pictures on detected faces (data/image_focus.json)
  • Keep real names out of the open-source app's comments (the demo's privacy check caught them)
  • Familypedia: curated articles for any subject, and wiki links that open non-person subjects
  • 0.8.0: published-sources and archive-open-book skills, curated Familypedia articles for any subject (documented), digitization work-spec template, LF line endings for CSV writers
