Transcription of the letter of Sept. 3, 1944 (Henrik Calder to his daughter Ruth). Typed by June Calder Moe.

Somewhere in France, Sept. 3, 1944. Dear Ruthie, Your mother says you have learned to walk the lunch pail down to the dock all by yourself, and that Grandpa Anders lets you ring the bell. I am very proud. Here we build bridges where the old ones were blown up, which is not so different from the dock, only wetter. Tell Pete he is the man of the house until I come home, but he is not to boss you. Your loving Papa.
