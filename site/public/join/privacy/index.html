Your privacy

DRAFT — plain-language summary, pending legal review.

  • Private by default. What you give us (your name, email and family name, and later any photographs or documents you add) is seen only by you, the relatives you invite, and Rex.
  • Never sold. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • Payments. If you pay, Stripe handles your card. Your card details never reach Lineage.
  • Emails. We write to you only about Lineage. Reply any time to stop.
  • Deleting your information. Email rex@verafy.ai and we will delete it.
  • Living people. Records about living relatives are never made public.
