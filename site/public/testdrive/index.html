Test drive

Type a family name and see what Lineage's research would start from. It searches three free public archives at once: historic American newspapers at the Library of Congress, books and local histories at the Internet Archive, and notable people in Wikidata.

Records up to 1949 only, and only people known to have died, so this can't be used to look up living people. Nothing you type is stored. Searches are free.

What Lineage would do next

These are only public starting points. With your own family's recordings, letters and photographs, Lineage reads every page, matches the right people to the right records, writes a sourced article for each relative, and drafts the requests to archives for what isn't online. Every email waits for your approval.
